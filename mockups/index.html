The Knack console, on three materials

Same content, same type, same motion. Only what sits under the glass changes.

  1. A, silk

    A slow gradflow silk field in ink, deep teal and sand, half resolution, almost no noise; the glass blurs a surface that moves like cloth.

  2. B, magma

    The gradflow eclipse behind the magma preset, recoloured ink, oxblood and sand and set off to the right; a warm rim the eye goes to first.

  3. C, still

    No shader: three hard-edged planes of teal, oxblood and sand, composed once, and the glass alone turns their edges into light.

Keys in every file: Space plays the block, A argues, Enter accepts, R replays. Screenshots are in shots/contact.png, the verdicts in NOTES.md.
